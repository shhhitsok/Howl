# HOWL — Wardogs Fire Control

<img src="logo.svg" width="96" alt="HOWL logo">

A phone-friendly mortar and artillery range finder for Wardogs.
Enter your X/Y and the target's X/Y, and HOWL tells you where to aim:
elevation (mils), direction (compass heading) and distance.

- **L81 Mortar**: high arc, about 80–684 m
- **SPH-2 Artillery**: low arc about 1181–2629 m, high arc about 780–2629 m

## Use it

Open the GitHub Pages link on your phone and tap **Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app.

## Notes

- Assumes +X = East and +Y = North, with 1 coordinate unit = 1 metre. Both can be changed in **Settings** at the bottom of the page.
- The firing tables assume you and the target are at the same height.
